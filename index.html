Our Story

Welcome to The Amber Canopy, where the flavors of autumn come together in a warm and enchanting dining experience. Our menu celebrates seasonal ingredients, comforting traditions, and the beauty of the harvest.

Harvest Moon Tart

Flaky pastry topped with caramelized butternut squash, crumbled goat cheese, fresh thyme, and a drizzle of dark forest honey.

A velvety, spiced acorn squash soup served inside a hollowed mini-pumpkin, topped with toasted pepitas and sage brown butter.
